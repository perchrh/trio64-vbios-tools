# The Trio64V+ ROM (765): 1.03-08N-p4

Our ROM for the S3 Trio64V+ (86C765, PCI 5333:8811 revision 4xh): the S3 BIOS 1.03-08 with this
project's changes, built by [`rompatch/vplus/s3vpatch.py`](../rompatch/vplus/s3vpatch.py). What of the Trio64V2 ROM's changes
([`docs/775-rom.md`](775-rom.md)) could go into it, and what it carries, was worked out on 30 October 2026 in the
emulator; the first card runs, on the STB Powergraph 64 and the Hercules Terminator 64, are of
1 November 2026 ("On the cards"). 1.03-08-p2 (1 November 2026, [`docs/765-changes.md`](765-changes.md)) fixes two faults of
1.03-08, removes the interlaced timings and moves the DCLK VCO of 10Dh-10Fh into range; it is checked in
the emulator, not yet on a card; p3 (#116) keeps interrupts off in the sequencer reset of 10Dh-10Fh.
From p4 (1 November 2026, [#129](https://github.com/perchrh/trio64-vbios-tools/issues/129)) the base is S3's 1.03-08N, the build theretroweb
lists for Trio64V+ cards, and the level is written 1.03-08N-pN; before, it was 1.03-08 from the GW503A
dump. The code is the same; "The reference image" below has the difference.

Scope: VBE 2.0 functions are not backported (linear frame buffer modes, 4F0A, 4F09, 4F06 BL=3 and the
other VBE 2.0 additions). The Trio64V+ BIOSes at hand offer VBE 1.2.

## The reference image

`reference/64vplus/1_03_08n.bin`, 32 KB, SHA-256 9f297e58..., S3's 1.03-08N as on theretroweb's
[S3 86C765 page](https://theretroweb.com/chips/3853) ("1.03-08N (09/03/96)", downloaded 1 November 2026):

- "S3 86C765 Video BIOS. Version 1.03-08N", "Copyright 1996 S3 Incorporated.", dated 09/03/96 (008Fh):
  S3's own BIOS for the chip, not Phoenix's, from the 1.0x code base of the Trio64V2 BIOS 1.01.04.
  theretroweb lists it as the BIOS for Trio64V+ cards in general.
- PCI 5333:8811, the ID of the Trio64; the Trio64V+ is told apart by revision 4xh.
- MCLK at POST by the strapped memory type: 50.11 MHz (SR10/SR11 = 41h/28h) for 1-cycle EDO,
  59.96 MHz (42h/41h) for fast page and 2-cycle EDO. 50.11 MHz is the stock MCLK of the Hercules and
  STB cards ("On the cards").
- No monitor sense at POST (00A0h bit 1 set): it always starts as on a colour monitor.
- VBE 1.2, so no linear frame buffer modes. 4F15h (DDC) times its delay with PIT channel 0.

Up to p3 the base was S3's 1.03-08 (SHA-256 0da79703...), the upper 32 KB of the Gainward GW503A
stock dump, not on theretroweb. Its code is that of 1.03-08N; 42 bytes differ, all in the header and the
configuration bytes ([#129](https://github.com/perchrh/trio64-vbios-tools/issues/129), with the disassembly and emulator runs):

| Offset | 1.03-08 | 1.03-08N | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0064h-0087h | "1.03-08" | "1.03-08N" | sign-on; the copyright text after it moves one byte |
| 003Ch | E6h 12h | A9h 12h | no code reads it; not established |
| 00A0h | 80h | 82h | bit 1 set: POST skips the monitor sense (124Ch, the call to B82h) |
| 00A8h-00A9h | 39h 42h | 28h 41h | 1-cycle EDO MCLK entry: 52.79 MHz against 50.11 MHz |
| 00ACh | 01h | 00h | bit 0 clear: when the sense finds no monitor, set monochrome and grey-scale summing and beep; never reached in 1.03-08N, whose sense is off |
| 7FFEh-7FFFh | 00h FDh | FFh FEh | checksum |

With the sense on (1.03-08), a display that does not load the outputs as a monitor does (an LCD, a
scaler, a VGA-to-HDMI converter) could read as monochrome and get grey-scale summing at POST; 1.03-08N
cannot. s3vpatch takes only 1.03-08N.

### The configuration bytes (1 November 2026)

From the disassembly of 1.03-08 (the code of 1.03-08N is the same) and POST runs in the emulator
(Trio64V+ model, 2 MB). The emulator always reads 3C2h bit 4 as 1; for these runs a harness modelled
the sense line for a colour monitor (every output loaded), a monochrome monitor (only green loaded) and
nothing attached (no output loaded), with the sense high when an unloaded output carries 15h or more.

- **00A0h bit 1: the monitor sense at POST.** POST tests it at 124Ch (`test byte [cs:0A0h],02h`) and,
  when it is clear, calls B82h, the only caller of the sense routine. B82h writes the attribute
  controller and the sequencer and calls BAFh, which shows black, blue 15h and green 15h in turn and reads
  3C2h bit 4 after each (C16h). Low on blue: a colour monitor, nothing changes. High on blue, low on
  green: a monochrome monitor, 0489h OR 02h (grey-scale summing). High on both, the pattern of nothing
  attached: the test of 00ACh. Bit 2 of 00A0h (the test at 1268h, around the video memory check at
  0E6Eh) is clear in both images.
- **00ACh bit 0: no monitor found.** Tested at 0BECh. Set: carry on as on a colour monitor, silently.
  Clear: 0487h OR 08h, 0489h OR 06h (monochrome display, grey-scale summing), a beep through the PC
  speaker (42F9h with AL = 3: port 61h and PIT channel 2) and the POST error word [bp+4] = 1.
- **003Ch:** the word after 003Ah, which points to the table of S3 register defaults at 00F0h (53h 00h,
  54h 30h, 55h 00h, 58h 83h, ...; the same in both). No code addresses 003Ch directly, and it is not a
  16-bit byte sum over any range of the first 1 KB (checked with 1.03-08, 1.03-08N, 1.01.04 and
  2.04.07). What it holds is not established; perhaps a value of S3's configuration tool. It changes
  nothing the ROM does.

BDA after POST in the emulator (0489h; 0487h is 60h unless given):

| Image | Colour monitor | Monochrome monitor | Nothing attached |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1.03-08 | 11h | 13h | 11h, no beep |
| 1.03-08 with 00ACh = 00h | 11h | 13h | 17h, 0487h 62h, beeps |
| 1.03-08N | 11h, no sense | 11h, no sense | 11h, no sense |
| 1.03-08N with 00A0h bit 1 cleared | 11h | 13h | 17h, 0487h 62h, beeps |

With the emulator's default straps the two images differ after POST only in SR10/SR11 (the MCLK entry at
00A8h); CRTC, GC, ATC, MISC and 0400h-04FFh are the same. Not checked on a card.

## The build: 1.03-08-pN

    make 765-rom OUT=S3VPP4.BIN             (rompatch/vplus/s3vpatch.py, 32 KB; SIZE765=64 for a 64 KB EEPROM)
    make 765-rom-test ROM=S3VPP4.BIN
    make emu-test ROM=S3VPP4.BIN CHIP=765
    make 765-ref-compare ROM=S3VPP4.BIN

Base: the S3 BIOS 1.03-08, "The reference image" above. A Phoenix Trio64V+ BIOS (1.01-05, 1.02-02) shares
S3's code for the VGA functions (the speed patch signatures match, checked 30 October 2026) but has its
own VBE and mode set code: the booster, 4F05 and mode set hook sites would have to be found anew, as
[docs/764-rom.md](764-rom.md) describes for the Phoenix Trio64 BIOSes. The Hercules Graphite Terminator BIOS
D2g (09/19/97, PCI 8811h) matches every signature of 1.03-08, at other addresses.

In [`docs/765-changes.md`](765-changes.md), each change with its issue.

The mode 13h scroll keeps EAX on the Trio64V+: the 1.03-08 dispatcher saves only the 16-bit registers,
so the 2.04.07 code (`p12ext.asm`), which uses EAX bits 31-16 without saving them, would hand them back
changed after every AH=06h in mode 13h. The emulator test `765_patches` checks it. The same code in
2.04.07-p20 did this through AH=0Eh, which bypasses the 32-bit dispatcher there; fixed in p21.

Checked in the emulator (Trio64V+ model): `make 765-rom-test` (the pinned SHA-256, checksum, every changed
byte at a patch site, the free bytes still 00, the refusals); `make emu-test CHIP=765` with 1, 2
and 4 MB, where every test that applies passes, `765_patches` and `stores` among them, apart from the
1.03-08 failures below; `make 765-ref-compare`: 2389 calls in the CGA, MDA and EGA modes behave as in
1.03-08. The S3COMPAT logs of the emulator runs (the whole S3COMPAT with 2 and 4 MB, its VBE part, its
VGA part) and the VSHOW /R log are line for line those of 1.03-08, apart from the sign-on. Test coverage
(`make coverage CHIP=765`): 87.1 % of the code, every byte of the new code among it.

## What p2 changes

The changes, each with its issue: [`docs/765-changes.md`](765-changes.md).

The free block keeps 610 bytes. The tests: `765_patches` checks each change with 1, 2 and 4 MB (4F02
refused exactly where 4F01 says unsupported; 4F06 keeps EAX-EDI bits 31-16; the four modes gone, also as
AH=00 78h-7Ch; 1024x768 and 1280x1024 non-interlaced at every CR41 index; 10Dh-10Fh with the VCO in
135-270 MHz and SR01 bit 3). `errors`, `windows` and `s3compat_full` pass without a note (`VPLUS_BASE`
is empty), and `make 765-ref-compare` finds no difference in the CGA, MDA and EGA modes.

## 10Dh-10Fh: the DCLK halved by SR01 bit 3 (p2)

1.03-08 runs the DCLK PLL VCO at 101 MHz in 320x200 at 15, 16 and 32 bpp (10Dh-10Fh): 12.62 MHz with
the post divider at its maximum (R = 3, divide by 8). The datasheet minimum is 135 MHz (PLL-1). The
Trio64V2 ROM raises R for the same pixel clock, but the Trio64V+ R has two bits (SR12 bits 6-5; divide
by 16 is a Trio64V2 addition, V2-PLL-3), so the lowest DCLK with the VCO in range is 135 / 8 = 16.9 MHz.

p2 doubles the clock instead: the refresh entries of these modes (05A2h, 05ABh) give 25.06 MHz (SR13 =
28h, SR12 = 61h, VCO 200.5 MHz, the pair of 640x480), and after the mode set `msx` (`vfix.asm`) sets
SR01 bit 3, the dot clock divide by 2, under a synchronous reset and with the sequencer index kept. The
CRTC counts the halved clock, so the timing stays: 31.3 kHz and 69.8 Hz instead of 70.3 Hz. In the
emulator S3COMPAT passes the three modes (DCLK 25.05 MHz, 69.8 Hz).

Open: the emulator and S3COMPAT model the CRTC side of SR01 bit 3, not the RAMDAC. Whether the Trio64V+
pixel path in these colour modes follows the divided dot clock shows only on a card: VSHOW on 10Dh, 10Eh
and 10Fh (a normal picture, or one twice as wide or broken).

## Does not apply to the Trio64V+

| Change in this ROM | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| FIFO fetch fix (CR90 bit 7 cleared in VGA modes) | CR90/CR91 (primary stream FIFO fetch) exist on the Trio64V2 only; the S3VBEFIX README names the bug for the Trio64V2 and the Trio3D. |
| CR23 = 00h before CR26 = 00h (p19) | CR23 and CR26 are Trio64V2 registers (datasheet section 4.2); 1.03-08 writes neither. |
| POST delay before the engine reset in AX (p20) | It concerns CR88, a Trio64V2/GX register; 1.03-08 has neither that code nor the BX mix-up. |
| DDC delay fix (p19) | The 1.03-08 DDC delay programs PIT channel 0 correctly (control word 36h), so it has not the 2.04.07 latch bug. Its one flaw: each 4F15h call resets the system timer to the standard 18.2 Hz, which a program that sped the timer up would notice. Moving the delay to channel 2, as 2.04.11 does: [#118](https://github.com/perchrh/trio64-vbios-tools/issues/118), awaiting implementation. |
| LFB base read and linear mode test fixes (p7) | 1.03-08 has no linear frame buffer modes (VBE 1.2): 4F01 returns no base, and its 4F05 and 4F03 do not test CR58. |
| Black level (blank pedestal off) | In this ROM it is SR1A bit 5 through the POST flags; the Trio64V+ data book mentions neither a pedestal control nor SR1A. |
| DCLK VCO at 170 MHz or more | The 170 MHz minimum comes from XFree86 and applies to the Trio64V2 only (V2-PLL-2); the Trio64V+ VCO range is 135-270 MHz (PLL-1). |
| 4F06 BL=3 maximum line length | A VBE 2.0 function, out of scope. |
| 1600x1200 at 60 Hz | The Trio64V+ reaches 135 MHz only with clock doubling (CLK-1); 1.03-08 ran 1600x1200 interlaced, and p2 removes it. |

## Depends on the card

| Change in this ROM | What decides it |
| --- | --- |
| MCLK 59.96 MHz | 1.03-08N picks its MCLK by the strapped memory type: 50.11 MHz (SR10 = 41h, SR11 = 28h, entry A8h) for 1-cycle EDO, 59.96 MHz (A6h) for fast page and 2-cycle EDO. 1.03-08-pN keeps both; `make 765-rom MCLK=... [MEMTYPE=edo1|fpm]` sets one, at most 60 MHz (MEM-1; README "MCLK build option"). Whether the memory of a card runs at a higher clock is decided on that card, with the VRAM test of S3COMPAT. |
| Memory size | 1, 2 or 4 MB in the data book. 1.03-08 sizes the memory at every POST (0E80h: 4 MB, then 2 MB, else 1 MB, written to CR36 bits 7-5), so a card upgraded from 1 to 2 MB needs no other ROM. The emulator tests run with 1, 2 and 4 MB. |

## Already right in 1.03-08

- 1280x1024 defaults to 60 Hz (108 MHz), 640x480 has 800 pixels per line (VESA DMT), 640x400 runs
  at 70 Hz.
- The 8x14 font is in the ROM (AX=1130h BH=2 and mode 10h pass).
- 1.03-08 has no 320x240, 400x300 or 512x384 modes, so the changes of this ROM to them (320x240 at
  60 Hz, CR50 for 512x384) have nothing to apply to; adding such modes would be a new feature, not a
  backport.

## S3COMPAT on 1.03-08 (emulator, Trio64V+ model)

133 passed, 6 failed, 3 warnings, 13 INFO. Of the failures, three are emulator limits that this ROM
shows too (104h and 106h judge planar memory above 64 KB, 4F05 the banked window: the model has
neither), and three are the low DCLK VCO in 10Dh-10Fh above. The warnings: AH=12h BL=20h (alternate
print screen, as in this ROM), 1600x1200 interlaced, and the LFB modes, which VBE 1.2 does not have.
4F14h (S3 OEM functions) and 4F0A are not offered.

## On the cards (1 November 2026)

1.03-08-p1 built with `MCLK=50` (`765M50.BIN` of `make roms`, main of 1 November 2026), RUNALL (S3TEST,
S3COMPAT with its VRAM test, VSHOW /R) on two cards with 2 MB, strapped 1-cycle EDO (CR36 = 92h):

| | STB Powergraph 64 | Hercules Terminator 64 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chip | PCI 8811 rev 40h | PCI 8811 rev 54h |
| MCLK | 50.11 MHz (SR10/SR11 41h 28h) | 50.11 MHz |
| S3TEST | 26 PASS, 0 FAIL, 8 SKIP (the Trio64V2-only lines) | the same |
| Banked writes (booster) | 79946 KB/s | 79946 KB/s |
| S3COMPAT | 136 PASS, 3 FAIL, 3 WARN | 135 PASS, 4 FAIL, 3 WARN |
| VRAM, all banks, 2 patterns | PASS | PASS |

- The three FAILs on both cards are FAIL 200 in 10Dh-10Fh, the low DCLK VCO that 1.03-08 has and the
  Trio64V+ cannot avoid with its post divider (p2 halves the dot clock instead, "10Dh-10Fh"). The WARNs: AH=12h BL=20h, 106h at 108 MHz (WARN 02) and
  1600x1200 interlaced (WARN 50), all as in 1.03-08.
- The Hercules card's fourth FAIL: "Mode 13h after 0120", DAC entry 7Eh read back wrong (FAIL 20), once,
  right after 1600x1200 at 134.8 MHz. The STB card passes the same line. The ROM's mode set code and
  DAC loading are 1.03-08's, unchanged; whether it repeats is open (a second S3COMPAT run on that card).
- Both cards boot Windows 98 and play video with this ROM. The wrong colours of the first video after
  boot that the Trio64V2 card shows with its stock BIOS as with our ROM (README "Windows 9x driver
  test") did not appear.
- The stock BIOSes of both cards run the memory at 50.11 MHz with this strapping (emulator, Trio64V+
  model, 2 MB 1-cycle EDO): the STB BIOS with SR10/SR11 40h 1Ah, the Hercules BIOS D2g with 41h 28h,
  the pair of our build. The Powergraph's manual gives 50 MHz as well. 1.03-08 itself runs 52.79 MHz
  there ("Depends on the card").
- S3TEST's timing lines on the two cards (mode set 03h 2.6-3.1 ms, 4F02 101h with clear 48 ms, 4F05
  24 us, mode 13h scroll 21 ms, mode 12h clear 1.8 ms) are close to the Trio64V2 card's with 2.04.07-p22
  at 59.96 MHz (3.0, 38.9 ms, 24 us, 20.3 ms, 1.4 ms). The machines may differ; VIDSPEED against the
  stock BIOS, as for the Trio64V2 (README "Speed"), is still to be run.
